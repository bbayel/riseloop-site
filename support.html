FAQ

Why does Riseloop need Screen Time access?

Apple's Screen Time is the only system that can truly block apps on iOS. Riseloop sets the schedule; Apple does the locking, entirely on your device.

I'm locked out and need an app urgently

Free tier: open Riseloop and use "Unlock anyway" (one slow breath required). Strict Mode has no unlock by design — in a real emergency you can uninstall Riseloop or disable its Screen Time permission in Settings, which removes all blocks.

My alarm didn't ring

Check that Riseloop has alarm permission (Settings → Riseloop) and that the loop is enabled for that day. Alarms ring in silent mode by design.

How do I cancel my subscription?

App Store → your profile → Subscriptions → Riseloop. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
