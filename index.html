Ottly

Earn your screen time. Ottly locks your social apps all day — and lets you earn them back. Focus for 60 minutes, get 10 minutes of social time.

How it works

  • Pick the apps that eat your day — Apple’s Screen Time locks them on-device.
  • Tap Start focus, or schedule focus blocks that start on their own.
  • Every 6 minutes of focus earns 1 minute of social time.
  • Spend 10 minutes once you’ve earned them — the lock comes back by itself. Unspent minutes expire at midnight.
  • Optional move rule: close your Move ring or log a 20-min workout once a week (Apple Health) to keep spending.

Ottly the otter keeps your stone of earned minutes — and your streak.

Private by design

No account. No cloud. Your Screen Time and Health data stay on your iPhone.

Ottly membership

Ottly is a subscription app: weekly, or yearly with a 3-day free trial. Everything is included. Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled in your App Store settings.
