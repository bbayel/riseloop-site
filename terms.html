What Riseloop does

Riseloop is a wellbeing tool that schedules alarms and blocks apps you choose, using Apple's Screen Time system. It is a commitment aid, not a guarantee: determined users can always remove restrictions by uninstalling the app or revoking Screen Time permission. Riseloop is not medical advice or treatment.

Subscriptions (Riseloop Pro)

  • Offered as auto-renewing weekly (with free trial) or annual subscriptions, or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed through your Apple account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store account settings.
  • Free-trial time unused when you purchase is forfeited. Refunds are handled by Apple under their policies.
  • Strict Mode intentionally removes in-app unlock options until the scheduled time — by subscribing you acknowledge and want this behavior.
