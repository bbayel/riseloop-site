Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Riseloop is built to be private by design: no account, no cloud storage, no ads, and we never sell data.

Screen Time data never leaves your device

Riseloop uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) to block the apps you choose. The list of blocked apps and all related activity is processed entirely on your iPhone. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We could not read it even if we wanted to — Apple's API keeps app identities opaque to developers.

What stays on your device

Your schedules ("loops"), streaks, morning stats, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third-party services we use

  • Adapty (purchase infrastructure): processes your App Store receipt to manage your subscription. See Adapty's privacy policy.
  • Amplitude (product analytics, EU servers): anonymous usage events (e.g. "alarm dismissed", "streak day 12") and a random identifier — only if you opt in (see below). No advertising use, no sale, not linked to your identity. See Amplitude's privacy policy.

Payments themselves are handled by Apple — we never see your payment details.

Analytics consent & App Tracking Transparency

On first open, Riseloop asks whether you want to share anonymous usage analytics. This is opt-in: if you choose "Continue without", nothing is ever sent to Amplitude. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Anonymous analytics, and revoking takes effect immediately.

Separately, iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, the device advertising identifier (IDFA) is used solely by Adapty to attribute which App Store campaign brought you to Riseloop — nothing else. If you decline, everything in the app works exactly the same. Purchases, restore, and all app features never depend on either choice.

Your rights (GDPR)

Analytics runs only on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings. We hold no server-side personal data about you. Analytics identifiers are random and can be reset by reinstalling the app. For any privacy request or question, contact benjamin.bayel@gmail.com.
